How to buy

  1. Add Wsy's BOT to your server and make sure you are the server owner or have the Manage Server permission.
  2. Run /premium buy and choose a plan.
  3. The bot sends you a private payment link. Payments are processed by Razorpay.
  4. After the payment is confirmed, Premium turns on for your server automatically. The bot sends you a confirmation.
  5. Run /premium status at any time to see your expiry date.

This is a digital service delivered electronically. Nothing is shipped. To renew, buy again before your plan ends and the new days are added to your remaining time.
