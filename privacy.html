Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what information Wsy's BOT collects when you use the bot and buy Premium, and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Purchase information: your Discord user ID, the Discord server ID Premium is bought for, the plan chosen, the payment link and payment ID, and the activation and expiry dates.
  • Server settings: settings you configure for the bot, such as your 24/7 voice channel, so the features work.
  • Discord data the bot receives to work: server, channel and member identifiers, and the commands you use.

We do not collect or store your card number, bank details or UPI ID. When you pay, you enter those details on Razorpay's secure payment page.

2. How we use it

  • To create your payment link and confirm your payment.
  • To turn Premium on and off for the right server and to show your expiry date.
  • To provide bot features, fix problems and prevent abuse.
  • To answer support requests, including refund requests.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. Payment details are handled by Razorpay, which receives the information needed to process your payment under its own privacy policy. The bot also runs on Discord and on hosting providers, which process data as needed to operate. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep purchase records for as long as needed to run Premium, handle refunds and disputes, and meet legal or accounting obligations. Server settings are kept while the bot is in your server.

5. Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you or your server by emailing versatilegaming110@gmail.com. Some purchase records may need to be kept where the law requires. Removing the bot from your server stops further data being collected from it.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, including verifying payments with Razorpay before activating Premium. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The Service is meant for people who are old enough to use Discord under Discord's own rules. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Email versatilegaming110@gmail.com or join our support server.
