Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

In short: if you cancel within 24 hours of your payment, we will refund you in full.

Cancellation and refund within 24 hours

You may cancel your Wsy's BOT Premium purchase and request a full refund within 24 hours of the time your payment was made. If your request is received within that window, we will refund the full amount you paid.

After 24 hours

Premium is a digital service that is delivered immediately. Once more than 24 hours have passed since your payment, the purchase can no longer be cancelled for a refund, and the plan stays active until it ends.

How to request a refund

  1. Email versatilegaming110@gmail.com, or open a ticket in our support server.
  2. Include your Discord server ID, your Discord username, and the payment ID from your Razorpay receipt.
  3. Send the request within 24 hours of payment. The time of your request is the time we receive it.

What happens when we refund you

  • We confirm the refund to you by email or Discord.
  • The refund goes back to the original payment method through Razorpay.
  • Banks and payment providers usually take about 5 to 7 business days to show the money in your account. This time is outside our control.
  • Premium is switched off for that server once the refund is approved.

Other situations

  • Duplicate payments: if you were charged twice for the same purchase, contact us and we will refund the extra payment.
  • Payment taken but Premium not activated: contact us. We will either activate Premium or refund you.
  • Misuse: refunds are not given when Premium is ended because of a serious breach of our Terms.

No recurring charges

All plans are one-time payments. We never charge you automatically, so there is no subscription to cancel. Your plan simply ends on its expiry date unless you choose to buy again.
